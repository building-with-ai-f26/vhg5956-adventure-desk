# Google career conversations: fictional demo

Create a new Research interviews adventure in the local app. Set title to Google career conversations (fictional demo), objective to learning about Google roles, career paths and breaking in, singular/plural labels to Contact/Contacts, and the four criteria to Role relevance, Career insight, Evidence quality, Warm introduction. Then paste google-careers-fictional.csv into Import records, preview and import.

All three people are invented and are not represented as Google employees. All emails use example.invalid and are non-deliverable placeholders. Open a contact to see the email and suggested discussion questions in evidence notes. This is an organization demo, not a verified contact-finding tool. No outreach or actual calendar booking occurs. Use a new adventure to preserve existing records.
